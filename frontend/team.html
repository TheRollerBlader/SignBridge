➔ GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Zero-Friction Accessibility

The application must work instantly on standard devices without requiring users to buy special hardware, download apps, or create accounts.

Speed Over Complexity

We dedicate time to collaborating to generate creative solutions where every team member has a voice.

Privacy by Design

Since you are handling live webcams, ensure no actual video frames are ever saved or sent to the server—only transmit the mathematical hand coordinates.

Accuracy Over Scale

It is better to have a model that translates the 26 static letters of the alphabet with 99% accuracy than one that attempts 100 dynamic words with 50% accuracy.
